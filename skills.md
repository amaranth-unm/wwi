# Gain New Skills With This Project
When you learn how to use GitHub Pages for a website like this one, you will gain a set of skills. Be sure to note these skills on your resume and during job/program interviews. Many employers will recognize the platform we use, GitHub, since it is one of the most widely used programs for app development. 

## You Will Know How Websites Are Made
This project gives you an understanding of the behind-the-scenes aspect of building a website. The more you know about how websites are made, the more you will be ready to jump into website work in the future. This will also give you practice working with plain text files. 

## Digital Storytelling
Presenting information on a website is much different than writing an essay on paper. You will learn how to adapt to this different format. In addition, you will gain experience writing for a broad audience. 

## You Will Have an Example of Detail Work
Many employers will consider you above competitors if you explain to them that you have experience with detail work. When you practice writing in markdown, which you will do to format the text and insert images for this project, you must remain attentive to small changes that might cause problems. While this is frustrating as a beginner, your problem-solving and troubleshooting for this project is something you can describe during job interviews in order to show how you respond to challenges and at the same time are attentive to detail.

## You Will Have Experience with Collaborative Digital Work
In many workplaces multiple people contribute to a project that gets presented in digital form. Experience with building a website with your classmates will allow you to arrive to future digital projects with a collaborative mindset.
